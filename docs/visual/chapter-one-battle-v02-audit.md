# Chapter One battle visual audit v02

- Evidence: `res://tests/evidence/production-battle-v01.png`
- Capture date: 2026-07-31
- Resolution: 1280×720
- Status: production-progress evidence; not a commercial-final approval

## Confirmed progress

- All seven Chapter 1 combatants now render imported character assets rather than greybox bodies.
- Player, enemy, Boss, terrain, cover, objective marker, HUD, lighting, and camera render together without missing-resource artifacts.
- Unit silhouettes are distinguishable by broad armor, mantle/focus, staff, degraded infantry, long-reach lancer, Ember Warden, and Boss profiles.

## Blocking visual gaps

- The default tactical framing is too distant: characters occupy too few pixels for armor, face, and weapon readability.
- Character geometry still reads as procedural/stylized blocking work rather than commercial-final authored models.
- The Boss is disproportionately dominant while several allied/enemy silhouettes are visually compressed near the centre of the board.
- Environment dressing is sparse outside the playable grid, leaving a large empty background and weak chapter-specific atmosphere.
- Material values are flat at this camera distance; formal texture sets, stronger normal detail, and controlled emissive accents remain required.
- Combat VFX, camera impact, secondary motion, and animator polish are not demonstrated by this still capture.

## Gate decision

The capture is accepted as truthful integration evidence only. It does not pass the 95–100% commercial-quality visual gate. The next visual pass must improve default camera framing, character screen-space size, environment dressing, materials, animation presentation, and VFX before another acceptance audit.
